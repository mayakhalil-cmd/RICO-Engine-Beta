# RICO

**Real-time Integration Control Operations.** RICO is a creative engine and
signal hub for Windows. Make a whole piece inside it, or let it sit between
your controllers, your creative apps and your hardware and make them talk to
each other.

Turn a knob on a MIDI controller and move a light in Unreal, a camera in
Blender or a parameter in Houdini. Send the visuals you make in RICO to
TouchDesigner or to another computer on your network. Plug in a homemade
controller and use it everywhere.

[![Watch the RICO Engine preview](https://img.youtube.com/vi/nZg4tgeFwy8/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZg4tgeFwy8)

## Download

**[Get the latest installer from Releases](../../releases/latest)**

1. Download `RICO-Setup-3.0.exe`.
2. Run it and follow the steps. It adds a RICO shortcut to your desktop and Start menu.
3. Double-click RICO to start.


## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Microsoft WebView2 runtime (already included in Windows 11 and up-to-date
  Windows 10; [download it here](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/) if needed)
- A graphics card with DirectX 11 support

## An engine and a bridge

- **On its own.** Make the whole piece inside RICO: motion graphics on a
  timeline, 3D scenes, sound, lights, projection mapping and LED walls,
  all running live and rendered from one place.
- **As a bridge.** Keep the apps you already use and let RICO connect them.
  A knob, a phone, a microphone or a sensor can drive Unreal, Blender,
  TouchDesigner, lights and hardware at the same time.
- **Both.** Build part of the work in RICO and send it on, or bring signals
  and video in from other apps.

## What it speaks

| | In | Out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIDI | Any controller or app | Devices, apps and virtual ports |
| OSC | Phones, tablets, apps | Many apps at once |
| Video | Spout, OMT, NDI (plugin), screen capture | Spout, OMT, NDI (plugin) |
| Audio | What your computer plays | Low, mid, high and beat signals |
| Lights | | DMX over Art-Net |
| Devices | MQTT (ESP32, Raspberry Pi) | MQTT |

## Highlights

- Workspaces you build, with panels you split, stack and share
- Routes that send one knob, OSC address or sound to many places at once
- Node networks for numbers, pictures, 3D shapes and text
- A motion graphics timeline with effects, masks, rotoscoping, custom GLSL
  layers and MP4 rendering
- Color grading with curves, levels and .cube LUTs
- An audio lab and a multitrack sound editor
- Projection mapping with surfaces, masks and lens gobos
